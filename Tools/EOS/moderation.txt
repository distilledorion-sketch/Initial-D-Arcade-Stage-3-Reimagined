Online bans
===========

The game reads active EOS sanctions for its signed-in player and verified
opponent. Administrator ban creation and removal stay in Epic's Developer
Portal. The player build never contains a moderation administrator credential.

Required client permission
--------------------------
In the game's Product Settings, select Clients (beside SDK Download &
Credentials). Under Client policies, edit Initial D Player Multiplayer.
Keep User required enabled and the existing lobby permissions. Enable only
Sanctions > findActiveSanctionsForAnyUser for this feature, then save.

Do not grant the player client createSanction, updateSanction, deleteSanction,
findAllSanctions or trusted-server permissions. The compact active-sanctions
read is sufficient to check both drivers.

Identify a player
-----------------
Open Online Battle > Player Details. Copy the online Player ID, not a driver
name, room code, leaderboard device ID or Steam ID. The panel includes your
own ID and the current or most recently admitted online opponent's name and
ID. Only one recent opponent is retained, in memory until the game closes.
Display names are labels and can change; the EOS Player ID is the ban target.

Create a ban
------------
In the game's Epic Developer Portal, open Epic Online Services > Trust and
Safety > Sanctions. Select the Live deployment used by this build.
Create a sanction for the copied Product User ID with action
RESTRICT_GAME_ACCESS, a reason, and the desired temporary or permanent
duration. Confirm it is Active, not Pending. The game also recognizes the
custom action ONLINE_PLAY as the same online-play restriction.

To unban, open the player's profile from the sanctions list. On its Sanctions
page, select the sanction's menu > Close, enter a reason, and confirm Close.
An active sanction's duration cannot be edited in place through the portal;
close it and create the intended replacement if a duration needs changing.

Behavior and limits
-------------------
Both actions above restrict EOS online play in this game. Offline play and
LAN are separate. Chat-only and other unrelated actions do not cause an
online ban. RESTRICT_MATCHMAKING is not used as a full-online restriction.

The game checks before online entry and before admitting a peer, then
refreshes active connections every 45 seconds. Known bans close the connection.
Short service outages may use a recent successful check for an existing
connection for at most 180 seconds; they never turn an unknown or failed
first check into permission.
An unban is recognized after a successful fresh query; Retry Online is
available after the restriction screen. Expiration comes from EOS, not the
player's adjustable system clock.

EOS Device ID authentication is account-free. These bans target the current
online identity; they are not hardware bans or proof of a person's identity.
The implementation checks both drivers, but modified clients and replacement
identities remain limitations of client-enforced, account-free P2P play.
The separate community-leaderboard moderation system is unchanged.

Administrative battle-stat resets
================================

An administrator can reset a player's online battle level/aura or full battle
history through the same EOS Sanctions page. This feature does not create any
sanctions or reset any players automatically. Create a reset only for the
intended player's current online Product User ID, copied from Player Details.
Both drivers must use an updated build that supports administrative resets;
older clients cannot join the updated build's EOS rooms.

Choose the reset scope
----------------------
RESET_BATTLE_LEVEL resets all 35 car models to battle level 1, zero battle
experience and zero win streak. This also resets the level-based aura. Battle
and win totals are retained.

RESET_BATTLE_STATS resets all 35 car models to battle level 1, zero battle
experience, zero win streak, zero battles and zero wins.

Neither action deletes cars, tuning, tuning points, save slots, Time Attack
records or replays. These actions do not ban the player from future racing.
Players can earn battle levels, aura, streaks and records again after a reset.

Create and retain a reset marker
--------------------------------
In the Live deployment's Sanctions page, create a sanction for the current
Product User ID with the exact action above, a reason and Permanent duration.
Confirm that it is Active, not Pending. Use a new sanction for every requested
reset, including a second reset of the same scope for the same player.

Keep reset sanctions permanently active. Do not close, expire or remove them
after the player reconnects. Each sanction's unique ID is the reset marker;
editing a reason or reopening the same sanction does not request another reset.
An active marker lets the game reapply the reset if an older record file or
backup is restored. Removing it can let an older restored file escape the reset.
Temporary reset sanctions are rejected with an administrator-contact message.

The existing findActiveSanctionsForAnyUser permission is sufficient. Do not
grant sanction creation, modification or removal permissions to the player
client, and do not ship an administrator credential with the game.

What the player sees
--------------------
The reset is applied when the updated game next successfully checks that
player's active EOS sanctions, before admitting them to online play. Existing
connections refresh their checks every 45 seconds; a service outage can delay
detection under the existing access-check grace period. An offline player is
reset when they next connect and pass the check.

If a reset is detected during an unfinished race, the race ends neutrally with
no battle result or race points awarded. The player must reconnect to race
again. A lobby connection also closes when its reset policy changes. A notice
explains that an administrator reset the battle stats and they can be earned
again by racing. If the reset cannot be saved, online play stays unavailable
until it can be saved successfully.

Each reset marker is saved with the affected battle records so normal restarts
do not repeatedly erase newly earned progress. Restoring an older record or
recovering an older backup causes any missing active reset to be applied again.
Previously recorded race receipts are retained to prevent old results from
being credited again after the reset.

Enforcement limits
------------------
Reset sanctions target the current EOS identity, not a person or hardware.
Replacement identities, modified clients and deliberately edited local files
remain limitations of client-enforced, account-free P2P play. Each driver
independently checks the other's EOS reset markers before accepting their
record state; this does not make local battle records server-authoritative.
Offline play and LAN do not query EOS reset sanctions. The separate community
leaderboard moderation system is unchanged.
