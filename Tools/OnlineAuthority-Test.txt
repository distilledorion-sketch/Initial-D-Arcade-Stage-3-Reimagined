INITIAL D UNITY - SEPARATE ONLINE AUTHORITY TEST

This optional build uses separate saves under:
%USERPROFILE%/AppData/LocalLow/Chris/Initial D Unity Online Test/userdata-unity-scene
It does not automatically load the ordinary game's profiles. Both drivers
must use the same complete OnlineAuthorityTest folder and build.

Internet play uses Epic Online Services matchmaking and P2P. Steam, an Epic
account and the Epic Launcher are not required. The publisher must configure
EOS before internet play is available in this build. Direct peer connections
are preferred, with Epic relay available when needed.

Launch InitialDUnity.exe, press F1, and select ONLINE. Use QUICK MATCH, or
HOST A BATTLE and share the room code. A private room is visible only to
people with its code. Choose cars and courses, select READY on both PCs,
then the host starts. LAN DIRECT also accepts the host's local IP and port.
The lobby reports measured connection quality once another driver connects.

Both cars share a verified input simulation with prediction and rollback.
The host can enable car collisions. Confirmed physics drives sounds and
results; clients do not submit authoritative car positions or finish claims.
A connection loss ends with no points or win/loss recorded.

Previous loopback TCP tests cover latency, jitter, packet loss, shared
contact physics, verified states, saved cars, shifting and disconnects.
Those results do not verify the new EOS integration or internet routes.
Use the current migration verification reports for the new transport's
actual test coverage; a local simulation is not a two-PC internet race.
