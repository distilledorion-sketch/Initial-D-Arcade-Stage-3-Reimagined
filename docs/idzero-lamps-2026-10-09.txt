Gunsai / Odawara night lamp presentation

Imported night scenery uses vertex colours plus point-light illumination.
The initial local lamp update incorrectly disabled the latter for IDZero,
leaving glowing fixtures but no streetlamp light on the road. Restore the
existing point-light evaluator for imported night road and scenery and remove
the disabling flag. Visible lamp lenses/coronas are a separate effect and
cannot replace surface illumination. Original D3, earlier imported tracks,
projected car headlights and lamp lighting on cars keep their existing paths.

Odawara's 105 PathV light anchors were imported only as illumination positions.
Recover their RGB and downward directions, plus the source LightGlare atlas.
Recover 1,320 actual lamp-lens triangles beside those anchors, using the two
authored lens UV swatches, face orientation and proximity. All 105 fixtures
have lens faces. This avoids making whole pole/railing materials emissive.
One small combined mesh draws white/warm lenses and a restrained corona with
depth testing, distance fog and independent main/mirror camera orientation.
No realtime Unity lights, shadows, bloom pass or per-frame mesh rebuilds.
Glow size/brightness are an adaptation; exact IDZero image parity is not claimed.

Gunsai's light PathV has only three black editor placeholders at y=0, about
29 m below the road. They are excluded from the visual effect. Its gate
lighting stays baked; no streetlights are invented at the placeholder positions.

Regenerate presentation metadata with:
  python Tools/Export-IdZeroLampGlows.py <IDZero package/data> RuntimeAssets
This leaves scene geometry, collision and driving data untouched. Only night
lamp-glows.json and Odawara's 131,200-byte source glare atlas are added.
Older course packs without this optional file still load.

Distance and uphill cutoff correction

Two further causes were reproduced in the production shaders. First, only the
eight lamps closest to the main camera lit scenery, so visible lamp pools
appeared/disappeared with camera movement. Second, the imported night shader
flipped authored normals against the eye. IDZero often supplies upward normals
on sloping road meshes; negating these at eye height produced a hard horizontal
cut through their illumination. Preserve authored normals on these surfaces;
retain geometric normal orientation for meshes without authored normals.

For IDZero night scenery, build a small immutable spatial index from lamps.bin.
Each 32 m cell includes every lamp whose 26 m influence can intersect it; there
is no nearest-light count limit or distance-to-camera switch. Evaluate the
existing smooth attenuation per fragment and keep the existing distance fog.
Odawara uses 1,188 cells, 728 references and 21,152 GPU bytes; occupied cells
average 2.54 lamp references (maximum 13), rather than evaluating all 105 lamps
for every pixel. No extra draw calls, realtime shadows or per-frame rebuilding.
Older packs without lamps.bin retain the fallback. Daytime and non-IDZero
course paths retain their previous behavior, as does near-car lighting.
