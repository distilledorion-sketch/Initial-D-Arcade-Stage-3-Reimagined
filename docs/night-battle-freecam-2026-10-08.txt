mouse/right-stick look is corrected; vertical look, forward motion, reset,
independent replay/HUD visibility, and elapsed-time movement remain intact.

Settings > Online > Lights-off Advantage: Hidden (default) / Visible.
Apply persists the choice; drafts and cancellation follow normal settings.
During night Online/LAN races, Hidden replaces the numerical advantage with
the original unavailable-distance dashes when the opponent's headlights are
off. The header, driver names, mirror, minimap, actual distance, race rules and
physics remain intact. Day races and offline battles are unaffected. Online
replays use their recorded opponent light state and the same local setting.

Existing original opponent darkening was verified, not replaced with a new
brightness effect. Night lights-off darkens the rival near/ahead of the player;
turning lights back on restores original ambient lighting. Original distance
fade and other car lights remain as authored.
