Odawara: Myogi speed with Tsuchisaka handling — October 9, 2026

Replaced Odawara's Tsuchisaka speed coefficient plus 1.2705 acceleration
multiplier with Myogi's original per-car/per-direction speed coefficient.
The acceleration multiplier is now 1. Steering, drift retention, braking,
cornering/contact loss and weather handling still come from Tsuchisaka.
Car-specific gear/RPM profiles and saved upgrade modifiers are preserved.
Odawara still owns its authored route, collision, two laps and course IDs.

The original speed coefficient controls physical gear speeds, propulsion
response and normalized speed/shift calculations together. It is selected
independently of the handling condition at session reset, rather than
scaling total velocity each frame. Solo and both online cars use the same
session path. Other imported and original courses retain their existing
coefficient selection.

Validation:
- 280 parameter combinations covering all 35 cars, both directions,
  dry/wet and tuning-table endpoints, with Tsuchisaka handling comparisons.
- 280 full-vehicle straight-line traces, covering every car, direction,
  automatic/manual and tuning endpoints: 3,395,826 simulated frames.
  Speed, gear and tach traces matched original Myogi exactly, including
  acceleration from rest and convergence to a stable terminal speed.
  This is a flat-road comparison; actual lap speed depends on corners,
  slopes and collisions. It does not give every car one universal top speed.
- Odawara and Gunsai road/collision/checkpoint/finish checks and 6,240
  driving ticks each across direction/weather/transmission combinations.
- Original data-reference and integrated driving-session regressions pass.
- Four online scenarios passed 115,200 confirmed peer frames with
  50/100/200/250 ms latency, jitter, packet loss and deterministic rollback.
  Two-account Steam/Unity transport was not newly tested.
