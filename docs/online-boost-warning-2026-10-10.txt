ONLINE BOOST OFF WARNING - October 10, 2026

Request
Display the original arcade Boost Off! warning during the two-car online
introduction, so players and recordings show the rule selected for the match.

Implementation
Reuse start2d chunks 81 and 82 (textures 11 and 12). The English and Japanese
warning assets are identical. Chunk 81 remains visible and chunk 82 supplies
the original dark flashing overlay for the first 10 ticks of each 30-tick
cycle. The counter is the original VS owner's phase counter, including its
reset when entering phase 1. Presentation advances at the existing 60 Hz rate.

Position follows the original 14D69A draw translation and 145EC0 bank matrix,
including authored depth. At 640x480 its bounds are approximately
(447.26,70.69)-(620.97,103.34). Existing UI fitting handles other resolutions;
imported course titles do not shift the warning.

beginVsBanner reads the initialized authority race's boost setting. It does
not guess from absent/default lobby state. Existing ready/revision checks
invalidate readiness when boost changes, and the race setup freezes that
agreed setting. Both local player slots and connected rematches use this path.
The normal VS owner removes the warning at introduction completion, before
the countdown. Offline introductions and replay playback do not acquire it.
No rule, physics, profile or transport protocol change is required.
