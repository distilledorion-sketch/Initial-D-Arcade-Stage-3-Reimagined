Odawara local leaderboard and best-ghost reset
2026-10-11

Odawara (course 17, conditions 34 and 35) now uses local Time Attack
revision 2. Its revision-0 and revision-1 rows are excluded whenever CSV
records are loaded, including restored older files. Gunsai remains on
revision 1; all other course revisions remain unchanged. All 35 car models,
both directions and both weather partitions are covered. Day/night and
transmission share these local ranking partitions, as before.

Game startup migrates these files before displaying local rankings:
  userdata/time_attack_records_v1.csv
  userdata/driver_profiles_v1/hakone_personal_v1.csv
  userdata/saves/slot_1..slot_5/driver_profiles_v1/hakone_personal_v1.csv
Save selection retains the same revision check, so restoring an old save
later cannot restore old Odawara bests. Successful migration preserves a
byte-exact .before-odawara-revision-2.bak CSV beside the original. Earlier
migration archives are not overwritten. A failed archive leaves the source
file intact while old rows remain excluded from the loaded record set.

Startup deletes retired Odawara personal best ghosts under the legacy and
five slot profile directories: ghosts_v1/course_17_{forward,reverse}_{dry,wet}
with either no revision suffix or _r1, including .tmp and .previous copies.
New best ghosts use _r2.idghost, allowing a slower first post-reset run.
The old per-car best telemetry CSVs named original_race_v3_odawara_* and
development_v01_odawara_* are also removed, across both routes, weather,
35 models and both transmissions. New Odawara best telemetry uses _r2.csv.
Cleanup retries safely on later launches and never removes revision-2 runs.
Unexpected linked paths or non-file entries are refused, not traversed.

Gunsai and other-course ghosts, tuning, car profiles, online battle history,
and optional replays/*.idreplay archives are not reset. Replay archives are
separate recordings and are not used as personal-best ghosts.

Verification:

  store locations, all car/direction/weather partitions, exact backups,
  restored revision-1 files, slower new records, retired ghost variants,

  Idas3OdawaraRecordChecks.RunReset exercises the actual native startup,

Rebuild Idas3Unity.dll and include it in the next player/update package.
