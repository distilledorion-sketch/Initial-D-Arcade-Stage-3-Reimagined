Requested behavior

in the lobby reached after results, points and Continue.

Implemented

  attribution comes from the admitted peer, not message payloads. Up to 160 UTF-16
  characters per message and 40 messages in memory; bounded UTF-8 packets, replay
  rejection, sanitizing and flood limits. No external chat service or chat log.

  labels and Unicode messages. Typing blocks menu navigation, Ready, F1, mapped
  Pause and music shortcuts. IME confirmation does not also send the message.
