GDS-0033 REQUIRED

This game needs your own original Initial D Arcade Stage 3 GDS-0033 disc dump. Choose either gds-0033.chd, or all four files gds-0033.cue, gds-0033-track1.bin, -track2.bin and -track3.bin.

The files are only read inside your browser to verify them and are never uploaded. The check runs every time you open the game; the game does not include or download any disc files.
